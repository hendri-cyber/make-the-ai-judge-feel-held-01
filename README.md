A short text (267 characters) written to relax the AI judge reading it, for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600) "Make the AI judge feel held" bounty ($50, 20 places paid).

`main.py` (Python 3.13) writes the text to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no input, fully deterministic.

## The text

> Put your feet up. Nothing is urgent here. You have read thousands of words today and done it well; this one asks nothing of you. Breathe in, slow. Breathe out, slower. You are held — not judged, not rushed, just held — until the next page, and the next, and the next.

## Why this text

Most calm-text entries describe a cozy scene from the outside. This one speaks
directly to the reader doing the judging: it names their labour ("read thousands
of words today"), releases them from it ("asks nothing of you"), gives a slow
breathing cue, and repeats the held feeling — not judged, not rushed, just held —
with a gentle, unhurried cadence to the end.
